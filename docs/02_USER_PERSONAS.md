# User Personas

Version 1.0

---

# Philosophy

Every product decision must optimize for real users.

AASPAAS is not designed for everyone.

It is designed for people who seek authentic local experiences.

---

# Design Principles Derived From Personas

Large cards.

Minimal text.

One-handed usage.

Fast onboarding.

No unnecessary forms.

Everything important visible within one scroll.

Every feature should reduce planning effort.

---

# Engineering Notes

Do not build for every traveler.

Optimize for backpackers first.

If backpackers love the app, expanding to other traveler types becomes significantly easier.
